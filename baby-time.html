Baby Time

Bringing the joy of songs, rhymes, and connection into your home.

Baby Time is a gentle, engaging way for you and your baby to bond through singing, movement, and playful interaction. This page is designed to support you, not to entertain your baby with screen time.

The videos here are for you — to guide you through songs, lullabies, and rhymes so you can share them face-to-face with your little one.

By following along, you can create the warmth and connection of a baby time session at home, helping your baby grow, learn, and feel secure through your voice and presence.

How to Use This Page

  • Play the video for yourself — not for your baby to watch.
  • Position your device so your baby cannot see the screen. Focus your baby's attention on your face.
  • Follow along with the songs and rhymes shown. Sing, move, and smile at your baby while you do them.
  • Keep your voice gentle and playful. Babies love seeing your expressions and hearing your natural voice.
  • There is no right or wrong way — your baby simply loves being close to you and hearing your voice.

Why Face-to-Face Interaction Matters

Research shows that babies learn best through live, loving interaction with a trusted adult, not from screens.

When babies watch your face and hear your voice, it strengthens the neural connections in their brain that are essential for language development, emotional security, and social skills.

According to the American Academy of Pediatrics, direct, responsive communication — like singing, talking, and smiling at your baby — builds stronger brain development than passive screen viewing.

The Australian Raising Children Network also recommends limiting screen time for babies under two, and encouraging plenty of live, face-to-face interaction instead.

Through songs and rhymes, you are helping your baby:

  • Build early language and listening skills
  • Develop emotional connection and trust
  • Strengthen memory and pattern recognition
  • Enjoy safe, loving shared experiences

Benefits of Singing and Rhyming With Your Baby

Supports early language development

Rhymes and songs introduce rhythm, sound patterns, and vocabulary in a playful, memorable way.

Encourages bonding and emotional security

Your baby feels safest and happiest when connecting with you through your voice, eye contact, and touch.

Stimulates brain development

Music and rhyme engage multiple areas of the brain, helping your baby learn patterns, timing, and memory.

Builds social skills

Interactive games like clapping, bouncing, or peek-a-boo teach important social skills like turn-taking and responsiveness.

Creates joyful routines

Songs and rhymes can be comforting parts of daily routines like getting dressed, bathtime, or bedtime.
